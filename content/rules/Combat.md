> Expect this page to get updated as we discuss further rules

In general, I love to see characters do wild shit and I will support cool play styles, so please let me know if you have specific character ideas. For example, I don't want you to base your character around always seeing the sun only to find out it might be a bit dim! This isn't to say I won't put you in sticky situations, but I don't want the setting to completely invalidate a majority of your character.

## General Rules

1. Rule of cool
2. Healing Potions
   - You can use your action or bonus action to consume a health potion or administer it in melee range. If you use your action, you heal the maximum possible. If you use your bonus action, you roll for the total
   - You can throw them in someone’s mouth to try and get them up, but it will be a high dc. Or you can do a lower dc to just try and hit their wounds to stabilize or give them successes.
3. To take the hide action, your target must not be able to see you. Also, if others can see you and are focusing on you, they might just yell out where you are breaking your stealth. (Sorry Dolan)

## Optimization Rules

This section is for the optimization rules. Feel free to skip this unless you are doing some wild stuff.

1. You can use your spell casting focus or component pouch as somatic components. This means you can use your wand and a shield if you have shield proficiency
2. Auras can only hit once on your turn then once off (i.e. Twice per round). This makes team play more effective but limits the overall damage.
3. Weapon Juggling: You can drop your weapon to draw your weapon along with the attack action, but picking up your weapon will then be an action. Normally, picking something up from the floor is an object interaction, but if it is for weapon juggling I will make it an action. This is to respect feats and abilities that allow you to switch weapons freely.
